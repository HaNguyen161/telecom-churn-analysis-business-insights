<h2>📌 Overview</h2>
<p>
This project analyzes telecom customer churn to identify key drivers of attrition and quantify business impact. 
The objective is to transform raw data into actionable insights that support proactive retention strategies and reduce revenue risk.
</p>

<h2>🎯 Objectives</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Analyze customer churn behavior and patterns</li>
  <li>Identify high-risk segments and churn drivers</li>
  <li>Estimate revenue impact</li>
  <li>Provide actionable business recommendations</li>
</ul>

<h2>📈 Key Insights</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>~20% churn rate</b> with significant revenue at risk</li>
  <li>Churn probability spikes after <b>4 support calls</b></li>
  <li><b>Network quality</b> is the main driver in urban areas</li>
  <li><b>Family plan</b> segment shows high churn</li>
  <li>Payment friction increases churn likelihood</li>
</ul>

<h2>💡 Recommendations</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Trigger retention actions at the <b>3rd support call</b></li>
  <li>Prioritize infrastructure improvements</li>
  <li>Introduce loyalty programs for high-value customers</li>
  <li>Encourage auto-payment adoption</li>
</ul>
